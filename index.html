1. Waste Processing & Langariyawas WTe Dispatch

During the current reporting cycle, the UrbanIQ heuristic TSP solver coordinated collection operations across 250 municipal wards in JMC Greater and JMC Heritage. Over 1,700 TPD of MSW is channeled into processing infrastructure, with 1,000 TPD supplying the Langariyawas Waste-to-Energy plant to generate 12 MW of green electricity, saving an estimated 11,480 kg of fuel and abating 31.2 metric tons of carbon emissions monthly.

2. Bisalpur Bulk Transmission & Loss Prevention

Bisalpur Dam storage stands at 314.85 m RL (94.2% live capacity), ensuring full supply resilience for over 14 months. The 106 km Surajpura-to-Balawala feeder transmits 710 MLD of treated water to Jaipur. Predictive acoustic anomaly analysis flagged 5 transient stress points, with valve throttling on Segment SEG-BLW-02 along Tonk Road Flyover averting fracture and saving 14,250 L/hr in unbilled water loss.

3. JVVNL Smart Grid & Industrial Peak Shaving

Automated demand response (ADR) modulated 86 MW of industrial load across Sitapura RIICO and VKIA cold storage clusters during evening cooling demand peaks (15:00 - 18:00 IST), maintaining grid frequency at 50.02 Hz and avoiding costly thermal peaker dispatches, generating approximately ₹38.5 Lakhs in daily wholesale tariff avoidance.
